# Mission

Current operational mission for the next 12 months.

Current Objective
-----------------
Build a stable, reproducible, repository-aware engineering cognition runtime capable of safe autonomous reasoning, repair, and durable sessions.

Near-Term Deliverables (12 months)
---------------------------------
- Month 1–3: Environment hardening, deterministic bootstrap, CI gates.
- Month 4–6: Repository cognition and symbol index; memory scaffolding.
- Month 7–9: Belief, uncertainty, and temporal planning prototypes.
- Month 10–12: Causal repair primitives and offline consolidation (dream cycles).

Core Problems Being Solved
-------------------------
- Environmental fragility and reproducibility.
- Hallucination and invalid edits due to weak repository grounding.
- Lack of consolidated memory and long-horizon coherence.

Success Criteria
----------------
- Clean bootstrap and reproducible dev environment from scratch.
- Repo queries answer symbol-location and dependency paths with >95% accuracy for core modules.
- Repair attempts include predicted side-effects and rollback plan.

Constraints
-----------
- Prioritize safety and auditability over rapid feature expansion.
- Maintain human oversight on high-risk mutations.
