# Physics Model

## Complex System
Physical systems modeling including particle dynamics and electromagnetics.

## Variables
- F: Force magnitude
- T: Time scale
- E: Energy conservation
- M: Mass/Density

## Failure Boundary
- Singularity points
- Conservation law violations
- Divergence of solvers

## Transition Point
- Laminar to turbulent flow transition (if applicable)
- Phase transitions in particle systems

## Stability Region
- Courant number < 1
- Energy fluctuation < 1e-6
- Convergence within 50 iterations

## Universality
Symmetry and conservation principles across different physical domains.
