Purpose
-------
Defines the central coordination arena where competing subsystems broadcast, compete, and win attention.

Structure
---------
- Attention Competition: arbiter and salience inputs
- Salience: scoring primitives and features

- Cognitive Coordination: orchestration policies and contracts
