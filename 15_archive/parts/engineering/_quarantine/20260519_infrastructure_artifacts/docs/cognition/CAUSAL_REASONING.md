# Causal Reasoning

Purpose
-------
Defines causal graphs, counterfactuals, interventions and how they are used for repair and planning.

Structure
---------
- Causal Graphs: nodes, edges, intervention costs
- Failure Causes: root-cause extraction workflows
- Counterfactuals: non-actual trajectory simulation
- Intervention Planning: candidate interventions and outcomes
- Dependency Propagation: how effects cascade through graphs
- Recovery Simulation: evaluate intervention safety and side-effects
