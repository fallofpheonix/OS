# Self Modeling

Purpose
-------
Describes the system's representation of its own capabilities, limits, and failure modes for self-awareness and adaptation.

Structure
---------
- Runtime Self-Model: performance, latency, accuracy estimates
- Capability Awareness: available models, compute, connectors
- Failure Awareness: unstable modules and historical failure rates
- Strategy Awareness: policy performance and trust scores
- Resource Awareness: budgets and limits
