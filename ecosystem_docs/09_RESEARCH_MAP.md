# 09 RESEARCH MAP

## Purpose
Overview of research domains and their interconnections.

## Domains
- **Cognition**: Understanding intelligence and thought processes.
- **RL (Reinforcement Learning)**: Agent learning and reward optimization.
- **Memory**: Storage, retrieval, and compression systems.
- **Simulation**: Virtual environments for testing and modeling.
- **Physics**: Fundamental principles and physical modeling.
- **Math**: Algorithmic and theoretical foundations.
- **Agents**: Autonomous and multi-agent coordination.
- **Neuromorphic**: Brain-inspired computing architectures.
