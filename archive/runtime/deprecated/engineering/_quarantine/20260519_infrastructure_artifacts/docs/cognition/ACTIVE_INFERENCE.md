# Active Inference

Purpose
-------
Defines predictive-coding style cognition: prediction, prediction error, and policy selection to reduce surprise.

Structure
---------
- Prediction: state forecasts and likelihoods
- Prediction Error: measures and thresholds
- Uncertainty: propagation and representation
- Belief Updating: rules and timing
- Policy Selection: action selection under uncertainty
- Surprise Minimization: objectives and constraints
