# Mathematics Model

## Complex System
Mathematical abstractions, optimization problems, and graph theory applications.

## Variables
- G: Graph connectivity
- P: Probability distribution
- O: Optimization objective
- D: Dimensionality

## Failure Boundary
- Non-convergence of optimization
- Graph disconnection
- Numerical underflow/overflow

## Transition Point
- Convex to non-convex transition
- Graph phase transition (percolation)

## Stability Region
- Condition number < 1e6
- Convergence rate > 0.1
- Dimensionality < 1000 (for dense)

## Universality
Scaling laws and structural properties of mathematical graphs and optimization surfaces.
