## Purpose
Tracking potential risks to the ecosystem.

## Risks
- **Dependency Risks**: Conflicts or obsolescence in libraries.
- **Runtime Risks**: Performance bottlenecks or crashes.
- **Archive Overload**: Loss of focus due to excessive historical data.
- **Coordination Collapse**: Failure of multi-agent communication.
