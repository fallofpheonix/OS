## Purpose
Management of installable and editable modules.

## Categories
- **Installable**: Pre-built packages and dependencies.
- **Editable**: Local modules under active development.
- **Runtime**: Modules required for execution.
- **Research**: Modules specific to research tasks.
- **Cache**: Management of module downloads and artifacts.
