# Phoenix.Cognition Ownership Graph

This document defines the strict ownership, purpose, and dependency graph for the subsystems within the `Phoenix.Cognition` (L5-L7) repository.

## Subsystem: `PhoenixMind`
*   **Owner:** Cognition Architecture Team
*   **Purpose:** The central AI orchestrator (L7/L5.5). Integrates telemetry, trace data, and formal cognition packages to produce actionable directives via the Oracle. Enforces Guarded Autonomy.
*   **Consumes:** `PhoenixCore`, `PhoenixGuard`, `PhoenixDistributed`, `Phoenix.Cognition/knowledge`, `Phoenix.Cognition/memory`, `Phoenix.Cognition/reasoning`, `Phoenix.Cognition/reflection`
*   **Produces:** Strategic Actuation Directives (via `PhoenixGuard/Warden`), System Advisories.
*   **Required by:** N/A (Top of the cognitive stack, optionally attached overlay).

## Subsystem: `knowledge`
*   **Owner:** Formal Methods Team
*   **Purpose:** Maintains the causal DAG and belief engine based on verifiable ledger events. Prevents semantic hallucinations.
*   **Consumes:** `Phoenix.Nucleus/ledger`, `Phoenix.Cognition/memory`
*   **Produces:** `knowledge.Graph`, `knowledge.BeliefEngine` (Deterministic World Model).
*   **Required by:** `PhoenixMind`, `Phoenix.Cognition/reasoning`

## Subsystem: `memory`
*   **Owner:** Core Infrastructure Team
*   **Purpose:** Provides formal tiered storage (Working, Episodic, Semantic, Procedural) and deterministic replay of cognitive state.
*   **Consumes:** `Phoenix.Nucleus/ledger`
*   **Produces:** `memory.TieredMemory`, Contextual Grounding.
*   **Required by:** `PhoenixMind`, `Phoenix.Cognition/knowledge`

## Subsystem: `reasoning`
*   **Owner:** AI/ML Engineering Team
*   **Purpose:** The agnostic inference bridge. Decouples PhoenixOS from specific LLMs while ensuring reasoning is mathematically grounded in current beliefs and knowledge.
*   **Consumes:** `Phoenix.Cognition/knowledge`
*   **Produces:** `reasoning.InferenceResponse` (Logic, Confidence, Proposal).
*   **Required by:** `PhoenixMind`

## Subsystem: `reflection`
*   **Owner:** Formal Methods Team
*   **Purpose:** The prediction and reality drift auditor. Verifies the accuracy of previous AI predictions against new reality facts to enforce system quarantines.
*   **Consumes:** `Phoenix.Nucleus/ledger`
*   **Produces:** `reflection.RealityDriftAuditor`, `reflection.Engine` (Quarantine Signals).
*   **Required by:** `PhoenixMind`

## Subsystem: `PhoenixMemoryLab`
*   **Owner:** AI/ML Engineering Team
*   **Purpose:** (CURRENTLY MIGRATING) The RAG/Knowledge vault prototype. Must be migrated to a formal SQLite-Vec backed memory schema.
*   **Consumes:** Unstructured data.
*   **Produces:** RAG Embeddings.
*   **Required by:** Nothing (Currently isolated).
