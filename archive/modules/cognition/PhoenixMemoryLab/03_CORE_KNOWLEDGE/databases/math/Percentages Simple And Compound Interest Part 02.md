# 12. High-Level Combined Logic

**Question:**  
A sum is invested at 10% SI. After 2 years, total amount becomes equal to another sum invested at 20% for 1 year. Find ratio of sums.

**Solution:**

First sum after 2 years:  
P₁(1 + 0.2) = 1.2P₁




Second sum after 1 year:  
P₂(1 + 0.2) = 1.2P₂

Thus, P₁ = P₂

**Ratio = 1:1**

- SI is **linear**, unlike compound interest
- Use **yearly difference method** aggressively
- Split problems into **time segments**
- Reverse questions require forming equations, not guessing
- Watch for **partial repayments**, most students fail there

# 1. Successive Year Interest Trap

**Question:**  
The interest earned in 2nd year is ₹480 and in 3rd year is ₹528. Find the principal.

**Solution:**

**Concept:**  
Interest grows by factor (1 + R/100)

Ratio = 528 / 480 = 1.1  
⇒ Rate = 10%

Now,  
2nd year interest = P(1.1)(0.1) = 480

⇒ 0.11P = 480  
⇒ P = 4363.64

# 2. Difference Between CI and SI

**Question:**  
Difference between CI and SI for 2 years at 10% is ₹50. Find principal.

**Solution:**

**Concept:**  
Difference = P(R/100)²

50 = P(0.1)²  
50 = 0.01P

P = 5000

---

# 3. Half-Yearly vs Yearly Trap

**Question:**  
A sum at 20% p.a. gives ₹8400 as CI in 2 years (annual). If compounded half-yearly, find extra interest.

**Solution:**

Annual:  
A = P(1.2)² = 1.44P  
CI = 0.44P = 8400 ⇒ P = 19090.9

Half-yearly:  
Rate = 10%, periods = 4

A = P(1.1)⁴ ≈ 1.4641P

Extra CI = (1.4641 − 1.44)P  
≈ 0.0241 × 19090.9 ≈ 460

---

# 4. Reverse Amount Logic

**Question:**  
A sum becomes ₹1331 in 3 years at CI. Find original sum if rate is 10%.

**Solution:**

1331 = P(1.1)³ = P × 1.331

P = 1000

# 5. Variable Rate Problem

**Question:**  
A sum is invested at 10% in first year, 20% in second year, and 25% in third year. If final amount is ₹16500, find principal.

**Solution:**

A = P × 1.1 × 1.2 × 1.25  
= P × 1.65

1.65P = 16500  
P = 10000

# 6. Depreciation Model

**Question:**  
Value of machine reduces by 20% annually. After 2 years, value is ₹6400. Find original value.

**Solution:**

A = P(0.8)² = 0.64P

0.64P = 6400

# 8. Time Doubling Trick

**Question:**  
A sum doubles in 5 years at CI. In how many years will it become 8 times?

**Solution:**

3 doublings → 3 × 5 = 15 years

# 10. Consecutive Year Amount Ratio

**Question:**  
Amounts after 2 and 3 years are in ratio 121:133.1. Find rate.

**Solution:**

Ratio = (1 + R/100)

⇒ 133.1 / 121 = 1.1

# 11. High-Level Logical Trap

**Question:**  
If CI for 3 years is ₹331 and for 2 years is ₹210, find principal.

**Solution:**

Difference = 121 = interest in 3rd year

Let P = x

x(1.1)² × 0.1 = 121

x × 1.21 × 0.1 = 121

x × 0.121 = 121

x = 1000

# 12. Fractional Compounding Trap

**Question:**  
Find CI on ₹8000 at 10% for 1.5 years (annual compounding).

**Solution:**

1 year → compound  
0.5 year → simple on new amount

After 1 year:  
8000 × 1.1 = 8800

Half-year SI = 8800 × 10 × 0.5 /100 = 440

Final = 9240

CI = 1240

# 13. Advanced Equation Setup

**Question:**  
A sum at CI becomes 4 times in 10 years. Find rate.

**Solution:**

(1+R100)10=4(1+\frac{R}{100})^{10} = 4(1+100R​)10=4

Taking approximation:  
2² = 4 → doubling time = 5 years

So rate ≈ 14.87%

# 14. Installment-Based Trap

**Question:**  
A loan is repaid in 2 equal yearly installments of ₹1210 at 10% CI. Find principal.

**Solution:**

Present value:

1210 / 1.1 + 1210 / (1.1)²

= 1100 + 1000 = 2100

# 15. Multi-Level Growth Logic

**Question:**  
Population grows 10% annually for 2 years, then decreases 20% in 3rd year. Net effect?

**Solution:**

1.1² × 0.8 = 1.21 × 0.8 = 0.968

**Net:** 3.2% decrease

# Final Exam Insights

- CI is **exponential growth**, not linear
- Always convert to **multipliers**
- Use **ratio of consecutive years** for fast solving
- Half-yearly and quarterly questions test **rate transformation**
- Difference (CI − SI) is a **high-frequency exam concept**
