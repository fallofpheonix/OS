### Heap

- **Top K / Kth Element / K Closest**: Use a heap to maintain the k most relevant elements efficiently.

---

### Greedy + Heap

- **Task Scheduler**: Use max-heap to always pick the highest frequency task with cooldown handling.
- **Meeting Rooms**: Use min-heap to track earliest ending meeting and allocate rooms.
- **Reorganize String**: Use max-heap to place most frequent characters while avoiding adjacency conflicts.
- **Huffman Encoding**: Use min-heap to repeatedly merge lowest frequency nodes for optimal prefix codes.

---

### K-way Merge

- **K-way Merge**: Use a min-heap to merge k sorted lists/streams in O(n log k).
