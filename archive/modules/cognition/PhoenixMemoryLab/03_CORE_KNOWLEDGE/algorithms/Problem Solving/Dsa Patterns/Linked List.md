### Pointer Techniques

- **Fast–Slow**: Use two pointers at different speeds to locate midpoints or structure properties.
- **Cycle Detection**: Detect loops by checking if fast and slow pointers meet.

---

### Reversal

- **Full Reverse**: Reverse all node pointers iteratively or recursively in-place.
- **Partial (k-group)**: Reverse nodes in fixed-size groups while preserving overall structure.

---

### Merge Lists

- **Merge Lists**: Combine two sorted linked lists by iteratively selecting the smaller node.
