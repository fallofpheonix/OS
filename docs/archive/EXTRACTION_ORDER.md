---\nStatus: Draft\nImplementation: 0%\nConfidence: High\n---\n# Module Extraction Order & Roadmap\n\n> This document defines the sequence in which subprojects will be extracted into independent repositories, ensuring minimal disruption and maintaining dependency integrity.\n\n## 1. Extraction Strategy\n\n- **Bottom-Up Extraction:** Foundation components are extracted first to provide the base for higher layers.\n- **Interface Stability:** A module cannot be extracted until its `contracts` are stable and finalized in the core repository.\n- **Zero-Dependency Rule:** Extracted modules must have zero dependencies on the internal packages of the monorepo.\n\n## 2. Extraction Phases\n\n| Phase | Module | Target Repository | Dependencies |\n| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |\n| **1** | **Contracts** | `phoenix-contracts` | None |\n| **2** | **Events** | `phoenix-events` | `phoenix-contracts` |\n| **3** | **Ledger** | `phoenix-ledger` | `phoenix-contracts`, `phoenix-events` |\n| **4** | **Runtime** | `phoenix-runtime` | `phoenix-contracts`, `phoenix-events`, `phoenix-ledger` |\n| **5** | **Warden** | `phoenix-warden` | `phoenix-runtime`, `phoenix-contracts` |\n| **6** | **Cognition Core** | `phoenix-mind` | `phoenix-runtime`, `phoenix-contracts` |\n| **7** | **Platform OS** | `phoenix-os` | All lower layers |\n| **8** | **Game Engine** | `phoenix-game` | `phoenix-runtime`, `phoenix-contracts` |\n\n## 3. Pre-Extraction Checklist\n\n- [ ] 100% Test Coverage for the module's public API.\n- [ ] All internal cross-module imports removed.\n- [ ] `EXTRACTION_READINESS.md` updated and approved.\n- [ ] Target repository provisioned and CI/CD pipelines ready.\n\n--- \n*Refer to [docs/governance/BOUNDARY_RULES.md](../governance/BOUNDARY_RULES.md) for modularization rules.* \n
