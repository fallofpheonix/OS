---\nStatus: Draft\nImplementation: 0%\nConfidence: High\n---\n# Cognition Confidence Mathematics (SPEC)\n\n> This specification formalizes the mathematical models for confidence scoring and propagation within PhoenixMind.\n\n## 1. Normalization & Bounds\n\n- All confidence scores ($C$) are bounded: $0.0 \leq C \leq 1.0$.\n- Values are represented using fixed-point `Dec64` with 6 decimal places of precision.\n\n## 2. Component Formulas\n\n### 2.1 Evidence Support ($E$)\n$E = \text{clamp}\left(\frac{\sum (w_i \cdot s_i)}{\text{Baseline}}, 0.0, 1.0\right)$\n- $w_i$: Reliability weight of evidence source $i$.\n- $s_i$: Significance score of the specific evidence event.\n- $\text{Baseline}$: Minimum evidence threshold for "certainty".\n\n### 2.2 Model Agreement ($A$)\n$A = 1.0 - \min\left(1.0, \frac{\text{Variance}(\text{Results})}{\text{Tolerance}}\right)$\n- $\text{Results}$: Numerical outputs from multiple reasoning engines or LLM runs.\n- $\text{Tolerance}$: Max allowable variance before confidence is zeroed.\n\n## 3. Confidence Propagation\n\nWhen a belief $B_3$ is derived from $B_1$ and $B_2$:\n$C(B_3) = C(B_1) \cdot C(B_2) \cdot C(\text{ReasoningStep})$\n- This ensures that long reasoning chains naturally accumulate uncertainty.\n\n## 4. Calibration & Weights\n\n- **Weight Selection**: Weights ($w_i$) are defined in `docs/governance/POLICIES.md` and can only be modified via Governance-approved events.\n- **Calibration**: Periodically, the system compares predicted vs. actual outcomes to adjust the $\text{Baseline}$ and $\text{Tolerance}$ parameters.\n\n--- \n*Refer to [docs/phoenixmind/CONFIDENCE_MODEL.md](../CONFIDENCE_MODEL.md) for implementation.* \n
